01 / Continuity

A little history goes a long way.

Each agent has its own conversations, workspace, and Codex memory. Shape its personality in an editable soul, review its reflections, and undo a change when needed. Follow a side question in a reply thread, add an emoji reaction on the web, or ask another agent to lend a hand.

Explore reply threads ↗
02 / A rhythm of its own

Make it a regular thing.

Turn a request into a schedule. Choose a model for each automation, set its timezone and date window, and keep routine checks quiet. Enable push notifications to hear when there’s a result or something needs your attention.

Your Roost, close at hand.

Chat, swipe into replies, share files, and return to your Feed and trackers on iPhone. The native app keeps drafts through interruptions and connects to your own Roost server. Build it with Xcode, or add the web app to your home screen. Pick a theme and settle in.

A small home.
Yours to run.

Roost is MIT-licensed and self-hosted. Your conversations, files, and agent settings live on your host. Connect your Codex account and start making it yours.

Built for one person. Protect access with native passkeys, an SSH tunnel, or an authenticated proxy. Your host needs to stay running for scheduled work. Codex and connected services process the data needed for their tasks.
