01 / Continuity

A little history goes a long way.

Each agent has its own conversations, workspace, and Codex memory. Shape its personality in an editable soul, and come back to the same familiar face.

Your machine. Your Roost.

A small home.
Yours to run.

Roost is MIT-licensed and self-hosted. Your conversations, files, and agent settings live on your host. Connect your Codex account and start making it yours.

Built for one person, with private access to the app. Your host needs to stay running for scheduled work. Codex and connected services process the data needed for their tasks.
